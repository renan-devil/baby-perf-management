# The Boy and the Nuts

![A little boy reaches up into a tall jar full of nuts on the kitchen table.](picture-1.svg)

Once upon a time, there was a little boy called Tom who loved nuts more than anything in the world. Crunchy hazelnuts, round walnuts, sweet little almonds. *Crunch, crunch, crunch!*

On the kitchen table stood a tall jar with a narrow neck. And the jar was full of nuts, right up to the top.

“Mummy, may I have some nuts?” asked Tom.

“Yes, you may,” said his mother. “Help yourself.”

Tom was very happy. He pushed his hand into the jar, all the way down. Then he grabbed as many nuts as he could hold. One, two, three… ten, eleven, twelve! His fist was as full as full can be.

He pulled. His hand would not come out. He pulled harder. *Hnnngh!* It was stuck in the neck of the jar.

![Tom tugs at the jar with his fist stuck inside, and big tears roll down his cheeks.](picture-2.svg)

Tom pulled, and tugged, and shook the jar. *Rattle, rattle!* But his fist full of nuts was much too big for the narrow neck. His lip wobbled, and big tears rolled down his cheeks. “Boo-hoo! The jar won’t let go of me!”

Can you guess how Tom can get his hand out? *(Pause, and let Constance guess.)*

His mother knelt down beside him. “Tom,” she said kindly, “the jar isn’t holding you. You are holding the nuts! Let go of some of them, and take just a few.”

Tom opened his hand. Most of the nuts went *clatter, clatter* back into the jar. He kept just three. And *pop!* Out came his hand, as easy as anything.

He ate the first nut. *Crunch!* Then the second. *Crunch!* Then the third. *Crunch!* And when they were gone, he put his hand back in and took three more.

> Don’t try to take everything at once. A little at a time, and you’ll get there!
