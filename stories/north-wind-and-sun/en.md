# The North Wind and the Sun

High up in the sky, the North Wind and the Sun were having an argument.

“I am the strongest!” roared the North Wind. “I can bend the tallest trees and blow up waves as big as houses!”

“Maybe,” said the Sun with a smile. “Let’s have a little contest. Do you see that traveller down there on the road, in his long red cloak? Whoever can get his cloak off him is the strongest.”

“Easy!” said the North Wind. He took an enormous breath, puffed out his cheeks, and blew. *Whoooosh!*

The leaves flew off the trees. The traveller’s hat flew off his head. But the traveller just pulled his cloak tighter around him.

So the North Wind blew harder. *WHOOOOOSH!* The traveller shivered, did up every button, and held his cloak tighter still.

The North Wind blew and blew until he had no breath left at all. “Pff… pff… I give up,” he puffed.

Then it was the Sun’s turn. The Sun did not shout and did not push. She simply came out from behind a cloud and shone, gently and warmly, on the traveller.

The traveller stopped shivering. He undid one button. Then another. He wiped his forehead. “My goodness, how warm it is!” he said. And he took off his cloak, sat down under a tree, and had a lovely rest in the sunshine.

The Sun smiled at the North Wind. And for once, the North Wind said nothing at all.

> Gentleness and warmth win where shouting and pushing fail.
